feat(timemachine): animated A-to-B time-lapse in the compare sheet

- TimeMachine component (compare sheet section): canvas animation
  cross-dissolving record A into record B at progress p -
  * pitch mode: curves aligned by relative duration (same grammar as the
    compare overlay), A fading out while B fades in over band background
  * vowel mode: scatter clouds cross-dissolving in the vowel plane with a
    growing dashed centroid arrow showing the migration path
- scrolling date badge (drawDateBadge): interpolated date + total delta
  days drawn per frame
- player UI: play/pause, scrubbable progress (0-1000), 0.5x/1x/2x speed
  (1x = 6s per pair), record button exports the canvas via
  MediaRecorder to a WebM file (vp9 fallback webm, auto-save on
  playback end, graceful toast when unsupported)
- pure frame math (drawTimeMachineFrame / drawDateBadge) separated for
  potential unit tests; i18n x5 locales
