test(audio): pitch algorithm benchmark on source-filter synthetic corpus

- pitchBenchmark.test.ts: 9-case grid (C3-C5 x clean/10dB-noise x vibrato),
  reuses the golden-fixture corpus quality (harmonic glottal source + Klatt
  formant cascades); reports RPA (<=50 cents frame share) and median abs
  error per algorithm; clean-corpus RPA >= 95% as a regression floor
- first-run findings documented in ALGORITHM-YIN.md "基准" section:
  clean corpus is a three-way tie (~4.5 cents); under 10 dB noise MPM is
  the most robust (RPA 100%), YIN suffers octave halving at G3/A4, and
  this pYIN's threshold distribution conservatively exits to null on all
  noisy frames - more gaps, zero octave errors, by design
- PTDB-TUG etc. intentionally not vendored (license/size); the CASES grid
  is the extension point for local deeper benchmarking
